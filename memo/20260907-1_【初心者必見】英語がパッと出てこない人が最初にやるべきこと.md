今日は暑いね。水必要だわ。

it’s hot today I need water

今日なんか疲れてない？昨日よく寝れた？

are you tired today? did you sleep well yesterday?

スマホの充電切れたもう誰にも電話できないよ

my phone died I can’t call anyone.

my phone is dead. I can’t call anybody

原型　過去形、過去分詞

die.     died.     died.

Dead:形容詞　形容詞の場合はbe動詞が必要

これ説明するの難しいなぁ

it’s hard to explain this

It’s hard for me to describe this

間に合わないかも急ごう

hurry up we may be late,

we might be late hurry up

your pocket has arrived

this restaurant closes at 9 PM we still have time

we still have time:まだ時間がある。残ってる感じの雰囲気がある時

we don’t have time:時間ないね

you can sit down the seat.

you can take this seat
