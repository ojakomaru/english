**Traffic, Trains, and Real English | How Native Speakers Talk About Going to Work

The United States version

so this morning wasn’t perfect, but it could have been worse. I left the house a little late and sure enough traffic on the freeway was already bumper to bumper. luckily I had my coffee and some good music playing so I just rolled with it. There was some construction so I tried to detour through the side streets not my best idea. I hit every red light. I seriously every single one, but somehow I still made it on time. I rushed in with my back and coffee acting like I had everything under control. Fake it till you make it.

The British version

so my commute this morning, wasn’t a total disaster, but it definitely wasn’t smooth sailing either. I actually got to the station with enough time to pop into the café. so that was a promising start, But then when the train arrived, it was absolutely heaving.I managed to squeeze on and just had to wedge myself between all the other commuters. The trains are hit and miss at the best of times, so I shouldn’t have really been surprised that we just ground to a halt about halfway there no reason given. Obviously, eventually, we pulled in and I rocked up to work about a quarter of an hour late, which is not too bad. all things considered.
